What this experiment doesn’t establish

A descriptive record of one run per arm and task. Not a confidence interval, repeated-sample result or general whole-team superiority claim. It does not measure revisions made after this checkpoint.

Lower cost across all skills remains unproven.

The eight-role integration07 comparison (2026-09-28, 1be35120) used 1.67% more summed tokens and 9.63% less CLI time. Only 2 of 8 roles decreased both. Bounded outcomes were preserved; unequal checks and recovered original output limit interpretation. These repeatedly exposed development tasks do not establish quality superiority or whole-team savings.
