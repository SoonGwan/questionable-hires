What this experiment doesn’t establish

A descriptive record of one run per arm and task. Not a confidence interval, repeated-sample result or general whole-team superiority claim. It does not measure revisions made after this checkpoint.

Lower cost across all skills remains unproven.

The eight-role integration06 comparison (2026-09-27, 1d0e92ac) used 16.95% more summed tokens and 10.00% less CLI time. Original review retains scope violations, unequal checks and output-recovery limitations. These are repeatedly exposed development tasks; the average reduction in this featured experiment does not establish whole-team performance.
