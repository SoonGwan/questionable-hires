# Exorcist efficiency candidate

Motivation: the [27-session transfer experiment](REPORT-EXORCIST-TRANSFER.md) tied at 9/9 for every condition while the expanded candidate used more tokens. This does not isolate the cause of token overhead; it does not prove every additional instruction or experiment is wasteful.

The next candidate replaces repeated general working agreements with targeted decisions: reuse reproduction evidence and harnesses, require an experiment to distinguish outcomes that change the next action, avoid simulating unavailable runtime evidence, and link rather than duplicate retained evidence. User scope, preservation, real-path execution, safeguard explanation, and uncertainty remain explicit. Description and invocation metadata are unchanged.

This is an unvalidated behavioral candidate, not a measured performance release. Shorter instructions do not establish fewer cumulative tool-loop tokens or faster completion. Existing old/candidate results retain their original skill hashes and are not evidence for this revision. The running HTTPX experiment uses frozen Con Artist and is unaffected.

Before claiming improvement, compare against the original and expanded snapshots with identical model/effort/environment. Reuse prior tasks only as regressions. Include a separate multi-step case where more investigation is genuinely necessary, so stopping early cannot win merely by doing less. Record task success, scope, reproducibility, input/cache/output tokens and wall time; accept no efficiency claim based on lower quality, omitted required checks, or favorable selection of runs. Keep ties and regressions.
